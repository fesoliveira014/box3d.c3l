The contact material callback now tells which surface it is answering for.

## Contact material ids

- `ContactMaterial` has two new fields at its end, `user_material_id_a` and `user_material_id_b`. They hold the user material id of the surface each shape touches at this contact, in the order of the callback's two shape ids. They are inputs: a write to them is ignored.
- A convex shape, or a child of a compound, gives its own material. A mesh or a height field gives the material under most of the contact's manifold points, and the lowest index when two tie.
- The struct grew from 24 to 40 bytes and its alignment from 4 to 8. No existing field moved, so a callback that never reads the ids behaves as before, but code that copies the struct by size must be rebuilt against this version.
- The callback still gets no triangle or child index.
- A world with no callback pays nothing for the ids.

Patches: `0004-contact-material-ids.patch` joins the series of v0.8.0. The archives are built from box3d 3362633 with the patches in `patches/series`.
